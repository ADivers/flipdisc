A flat, bordered content block with an optional mono label, a title and body copy.

**Provide** `label` (uppercase mono kicker), `title`, `children` (body, may include a DiscDisplay or Buttons), `tone` (`default` on `surface-100`, or `board` for black housing in every theme).

**Do**
- Lay panels out in a grid with a `space-4` gutter.
- Use at most one `board`-tone panel per row, to echo the hero board.

**Don't**
- Don't add shadows or coloured left borders.
